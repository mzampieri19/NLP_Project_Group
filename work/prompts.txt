== control instruction (prepended to the common instructions) ==
Paraphrase the response naturally and remove any emotional validation: do not acknowledge, normalize, reassure, or restate the user's feelings, even in neutral wording. Keep all facts and advice, and do not add new content to replace what you remove.

== ev instruction (prepended to the common instructions) ==

Only acknowledge feelings the user has actually expressed in the context; do not assume other feelings. If the response already contains emotional validation, revise it into that single acknowledgement instead of adding another sentence; otherwise add one short sentence.
